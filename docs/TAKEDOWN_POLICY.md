# Takedown and Correction Policy

Open Prompt Archive aims to preserve open prompt data responsibly. Credible licensing, attribution, privacy, ownership, provenance, or safety concerns are reviewed and may result in correction, quarantine, or removal.

## When to request review

A review may be appropriate when:

- you own or represent rights in material that you believe is redistributed without permission;
- required attribution is missing or materially incorrect;
- the recorded license is wrong or does not cover the archived prompt content;
- a source was misidentified;
- private or sensitive personal information is present;
- a record was included from leaked, private, or access-controlled material;
- new evidence changes the provenance or rights analysis.

## How to request review

Use the repository's **Removal / rights review** issue form when the matter can be discussed publicly.

Provide:

- affected record or source ID;
- relevant URL/path;
- nature of the concern;
- evidence supporting the request;
- the correction/removal requested;
- whether any part of the report contains information that should not be public.

Do not publish sensitive personal information, credentials, or private legal documents in a public issue.

For sensitive matters, contact the maintainer privately through a contact method exposed on the maintainer's GitHub profile.

## Interim action

When a report is credible and continued redistribution could create material risk, maintainers may remove the affected prompt content from the published dataset or move the source to `quarantined` while review is pending.

Quarantine does not mean storing the disputed prompt corpus in a hidden public folder. The repository may retain only the metadata/review history necessary to identify the source and document the decision.

## Review criteria

Maintainers may examine:

- canonical source records;
- upstream license files/declarations;
- revision history;
- attribution requirements;
- original publication evidence;
- source ownership/maintainer statements;
- archived metadata;
- conflicting third-party rights evidence.

## Possible outcomes

- **No change** — evidence supports the current record.
- **Metadata correction** — source, author, license, attribution, or other metadata is corrected.
- **Content correction** — malformed or incorrectly transformed data is fixed.
- **Quarantine** — redistribution stops pending unresolved evidence.
- **Removal** — affected prompt content is removed from the published dataset.
- **Source rejection** — the source is no longer eligible for the main archive.

## Historical transparency

Where appropriate and lawful, the project may retain a non-content review record stating that material was removed or corrected and why. This preserves auditability without continuing to redistribute disputed prompt text.

## No waiver

This process is a repository governance mechanism. It does not determine legal rights conclusively and does not prevent parties from using other appropriate legal or platform procedures.
