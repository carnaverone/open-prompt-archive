# Source Review — YouMind OpenLab / Awesome Nano Banana Pro Prompts

- **Source ID:** `youmind-nano-banana-pro`
- **Canonical repository:** `https://github.com/YouMind-OpenLab/awesome-nano-banana-pro-prompts`
- **Upstream owner:** YouMind OpenLab
- **Reviewed revision:** `7833dde87d4f955c274d95186c4a49836520495f`
- **Review date:** 2026-08-23
- **Decision:** `quarantined`
- **Claimed upstream license:** `CC-BY-4.0`
- **Import status:** no prompt corpus may be redistributed by Open Prompt Archive at this stage

## Evidence reviewed

1. The repository contains an explicit CC BY 4.0 license statement naming YouMind OpenLab.
2. The README states that the prompts are **collected from the community for educational purposes** and provides a takedown mechanism.
3. Prompt entries may identify external original authors and link to external social-media posts.
4. The contribution guide states that contributors agree to CC BY 4.0 and confirm that they have the right to submit the content.
5. The same contribution guide also permits material described as `Original or properly attributed`; attribution alone does not establish redistribution authority for third-party material.

## Scope concern

The repository-level CC BY 4.0 declaration is clear, but the reviewed evidence does not establish that **every externally collected prompt** was submitted by its rights holder or otherwise licensed to YouMind OpenLab under terms that permit YouMind OpenLab to relicense it under CC BY 4.0.

This distinction matters because some entries appear to originate from unrelated community authors on external platforms. A repository license cannot by itself resolve third-party rights when the corpus is an aggregation.

## Quarantine rule

Open Prompt Archive may retain source metadata and this review, but must not copy the bulk prompt corpus while this scope question remains unresolved.

A future subset may be eligible if record-level evidence demonstrates one of the following:

- the original author submitted the prompt under YouMind's CC BY contribution agreement;
- the original author separately licensed the prompt under compatible terms;
- YouMind provides authoritative evidence that the relevant record is covered by its CC BY grant;
- the prompt is otherwise demonstrably public domain or openly licensed by the rights holder.

Associated images/media remain out of scope regardless of prompt-text resolution unless separately reviewed.

## Re-review requirements

To move this source to `approved`, obtain evidence establishing license authority for the specific corpus or a reliably identifiable subset of records.

## Conclusion

**QUARANTINED for bulk import.** The repository claims CC BY 4.0, but current evidence does not sufficiently verify license scope over all externally collected prompt text.
