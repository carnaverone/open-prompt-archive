## Evidence reviewed

1. The repository contains an explicit CC BY 4.0 license statement naming YouMind OpenLab.
2. The README states that prompts are collected from the community for educational purposes and offers a rights/takedown mechanism.
3. The repository's contribution guide says contributors agree to CC BY 4.0 and confirm they have the right to submit content.
4. The contribution guide also permits prompts described as `Original or properly attributed`, while prompt records may identify external authors and external social-media sources.

## Scope concern

The CC BY 4.0 declaration is explicit at repository level, but the reviewed evidence does not establish that every externally collected prompt was submitted by the original rights holder or otherwise licensed to YouMind OpenLab with authority to place that prompt under CC BY 4.0.

Attribution to an external author is useful provenance but does not itself grant redistribution or sublicensing rights.

## Quarantine rule

Open Prompt Archive may retain metadata about this source and the review decision, but must not copy the bulk prompt corpus until the licensing scope is resolved.

A record-level subset may later be eligible when authoritative evidence shows that the specific prompt was:

- submitted by its original author under the YouMind CC BY contribution agreement;
- independently published by its author under a compatible open license;
- expressly licensed to YouMind with redistribution authority; or
- otherwise demonstrably public-domain/openly licensed material.

Associated images and generated media are not covered by this prompt-text review and must not be mirrored without separate evidence.

## Re-review requirements

To move the bulk source to `approved`, obtain reliable evidence connecting the repository's CC BY license to the rights status of the externally collected prompt corpus, or define a mechanically identifiable subset for which that connection can be verified.

## Conclusion

**QUARANTINED for bulk import.** The repository claims CC BY 4.0, but license authority over the complete externally collected prompt corpus is not sufficiently verified for Open Prompt Archive's provenance standard.
