## Evidence reviewed

1. The repository contains an explicit CC BY 4.0 license statement naming YouMind OpenLab.
2. The README states that the prompt collection is gathered from the community for educational purposes and provides a takedown route for rights concerns.
3. Prompt entries are presented as a curated community collection rather than a corpus demonstrated to be authored entirely by YouMind OpenLab.
4. At the reviewed revision, no contribution document was located that establishes a record-level rights grant covering the complete externally collected corpus.

## Scope concern

A repository-level CC BY declaration does not by itself prove that YouMind OpenLab holds the rights necessary to license every prompt collected from unrelated community authors.

Open Prompt Archive requires evidence that the claimed license actually covers the prompt text being redistributed, not merely the repository wrapper or curation work.

## Quarantine rule

Open Prompt Archive may retain source metadata and this review, but must not redistribute the bulk prompt corpus while license authority over externally sourced records remains unresolved.

A record-level subset can be reconsidered if authoritative evidence demonstrates that the original author licensed the prompt under compatible terms or directly submitted it under an enforceable open-license contribution agreement.

Associated videos, thumbnails, images, audio, likenesses, and other media remain excluded unless separately reviewed.

## Re-review requirements

To move this source to `approved`, establish one of the following:

- authoritative coverage of the corpus by YouMind's CC BY license;
- record-level rights grants from original authors;
- an identifiable subset consisting only of prompts with independently verified open licenses or public-domain status.

## Conclusion

**QUARANTINED for bulk import.** The repository states CC BY 4.0, but current evidence does not verify that the license covers all community-sourced prompt text to Open Prompt Archive's required standard.
