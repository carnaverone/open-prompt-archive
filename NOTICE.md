# Notice and Attribution

Open Prompt Archive contains two distinct classes of material:

1. **Repository-authored files** created for this project, including documentation, schemas, templates, and supporting tooling.
2. **Imported prompt/data records** originating from third-party sources.

These classes are licensed separately.

## Repository-authored material

Repository-authored files are covered by the project's applicable repository license unless a file states otherwise. SPDX/REUSE-compatible metadata may also be used to make that licensing machine-readable.

The root `LICENSE` does **not** automatically relicense imported third-party prompt/data content.

## Imported prompt data

Every accepted third-party record must preserve, directly or through its approved source registry entry:

- source name and stable source ID;
- canonical source URL;
- upstream author/maintainer when known or required;
- effective upstream license identifier;
- direct license evidence where available;
- attribution text when required;
- retrieval/review date;
- source revision/commit/tag/snapshot when available;
- provenance/integrity information;
- modification state when prompt text was changed.

Attribution and license metadata for imported data are derived from `sources/sources.yaml` and the individual prompt records. Downstream users remain responsible for complying with those upstream terms.

## Associated media

A prompt-text license does not automatically establish redistribution rights for associated images, video, audio, likenesses, trademarks, or other media.

Third-party media is not mirrored by default and requires independent rights verification.

## Quarantined or disputed sources

A source with unresolved rights or provenance concerns may retain a metadata/review record so that the decision remains auditable. The disputed prompt corpus must not remain publicly redistributed merely under a `quarantined` label.

## Corrections

If you believe a source, attribution, license classification, provenance claim, or record is incorrect, use the repository's license/provenance correction or removal/rights-review issue form and provide primary evidence where possible.

See [`docs/TAKEDOWN_POLICY.md`](docs/TAKEDOWN_POLICY.md).
