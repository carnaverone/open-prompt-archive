# Code of Conduct

Open Prompt Archive is a public data-curation project. Participation is expected to remain professional, evidence-based, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

Contributors and maintainers should:

- discuss people, projects, and licensing disputes without personal attacks;
- distinguish verified evidence from assumptions or opinions;
- give proper credit to upstream authors and maintainers;
- respect privacy and avoid publishing unnecessary personal information;
- disclose uncertainty when provenance or licensing cannot be established;
- accept good-faith corrections when stronger evidence becomes available;
- keep technical and rights-related discussions focused on the repository's curation goals.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory abuse;
- doxxing or publication of private personal information;
- knowingly false claims about authorship, ownership, licensing, or provenance;
- repeated attempts to reintroduce content that has been removed or quarantined for unresolved rights concerns without new evidence;
- spam, deceptive promotion, or unrelated solicitation;
- deliberately submitting leaked, private, credential-gated, or unlawfully obtained material;
- retaliation against people who report credible licensing, attribution, privacy, or data-quality concerns.

## Evidence disputes

Licensing and provenance questions can be contested without being interpersonal conflicts.

When evidence conflicts, participants should cite the canonical source, exact license text or declaration, relevant revision, and any other primary evidence available. Maintainers may quarantine content while a dispute is reviewed.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this policy. Depending on severity, maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, reject contributions, restrict participation, or report abuse through GitHub's platform mechanisms.

For non-sensitive conduct concerns, contact the maintainers through the repository's GitHub discussion/issue mechanisms and clearly mark the report as a conduct concern.

For sensitive matters, avoid posting private evidence publicly. Contact the repository maintainer through the maintainer's GitHub profile or other private contact method made available there.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, review comments, contribution discussions, and other project spaces managed by Open Prompt Archive.
